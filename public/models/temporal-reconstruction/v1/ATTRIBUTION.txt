Changes: subdivision and mild Taubin smoothing; small styloid tip deformation;
15 finite cylindrical recesses representing approximate canal entrances;
remapped and expanded surface masks. Built with scripts/reconstruct-temporal.py.
These changes do not constitute a new anatomical scan or expert validation.
Subdivision increases polygon count, not anatomical evidence.

Reference consultation (no images or meshes copied):
https://www.anatomystandard.com/ossa-et-juncturae/cranium/os-temporale.html
(right bone atlas; anatomical relationships interpreted for left side)
https://www.anatomyatlases.org/atlasofanatomy/plate02/03ltemporalouter.shtml
(left temporal bone, outer surface)

All 23 regions and all authored recess dimensions/locations are approximate.
Recesses are blind schematic entrances, NOT complete acoustic/carotid/facial
canals. Do not infer canal connectivity or precise measurements.
The original whole bone and OpenEar specimen remain separately available.
Only a subset of labels is transferred to this prototype.

Stage 3 adds canals.json: 12 authored schematic lumen centerlines with radii.
Generated by scripts/build-temporal-canals.py. These are colored illustrative
casts, not segmented canal walls or verified intraosseous paths. Branches,
bends, diameters, and terminations remain approximate. The chorda tympani
trajectory includes a free tympanic segment and is not one continuous bony canal.
The slice clips the bone shell only; illustrative canal paths remain uncut.
No histological section surface is reconstructed.

Stage 4 adds approximate volume casts for the tympanic cavity and vestibule,
a 2.5-turn cochlear spiral, three semicircular canals with schematic ampullae,
and their common bony crus. Ellipsoids and centerline sweeps are teaching
approximations, not anatomical segmentation. Ossicles, windows, detailed cavity
walls, cochlear scalae, modiolus and membranous labyrinth are not modeled.
Additional reference: Hoang & Loevner, Evaluation of Tinnitus and Hearing Loss
in the Adult, anatomy section, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK554328/
Rebuild in order: reconstruct-temporal.py, build-temporal-canals.py,
build-temporal-labyrinth.py. All run locally; no atlas images/meshes copied.

Stage 5: run build-temporal-middle-ear.py after the preceding scripts.
Adds 3 compound schematic ossicles, 2 window rims and 6 curved wall sectors.
Wall sectors follow the existing ellipsoidal cavity approximation; they are not
validated anatomy. The labyrinthine sector has schematic window openings.
The membranous wall is not represented as solid bone. Fine relief, ligaments,
membranes, mastoid aditus and anterior wall canal openings remain incomplete.
Sources consulted:
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK482338/
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6081290/
All new components carry explicit approximate reconstruction labels.

Stage 6: run build-temporal-mastoid.py after build-temporal-middle-ear.py.
Adds an illustrative antrum, aditus from the epitympanum, and 12 connected
ellipsoidal air-space volumes representing mastoid cells as one selectable group.
The number and arrangement are illustrative, not normative or patient-specific.
A schematic opening is removed from wall sectors along the aditus centerline.
Fine bony septa, mucosa and an anatomically validated pneumatization pattern
are not modeled. Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6081290/

Stage 7: run build-temporal-muscles.py after build-temporal-mastoid.py.
Adds a conical tympanic membrane sheet, tensor tympani and stapedius muscle
schematics and two separately selectable tendons. Attachments refer to this
project's approximate ossicles. The umbo meets the modeled malleus handle tip.
Membrane layers/pars flaccida, physiological motion, precise muscle origins,
cochleariform process relief and pyramidal eminence housing are not recreated.
The tensor canal cast is hidden while selecting its muscle/tendon to expose it.
Sources: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/68013719
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/68013198
These soft tissues are schematic tissue shapes, not casts of air spaces.

Stage 8: run build-temporal-nerves.py after build-temporal-muscles.py.
Adds intratemporal facial nerve, chorda tympani and vestibulocochlear nerve
with illustrative cochlear and superior/inferior vestibular branches.
Uses atlas relationships only; geometry is authored locally. No source images
or meshes copied. Sites consulted:
https://meduniver.com/Medical/Anatom/472.html
https://meduniver.com/Medical/Anatom/605.html
https://meduniver.com/Medical/Anatom/preddverno-ulitkovii_nerv.html
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8791922/
Precise nerve positions, diameter and terminal fields are unvalidated.
Ganglia, intermediate nerve, brainstem nuclei and extracranial continuations
are not reconstructed. Corresponding lumen casts hide on nerve selection.

Stage 9: build-temporal-nerves.py now also generates a schematic geniculate
ganglion at the first facial bend, a greater petrosal segment to its hiatus,
and an intermediate nerve root between the modeled VII and VIII roots.
Their shape and attachment coordinates remain approximate. The distal greater
petrosal course, deep petrosal junction, pterygoid canal and individual fiber
courses are not reconstructed. Motor fibers are not represented as synapsing
in the sensory ganglion. Additional reference consulted:
https://meduniver.com/Medical/otorinolaringologia_bolezni_lor_organov/xirurgicheskaia_anatomia_nervov_uxa.html
