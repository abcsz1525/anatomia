https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy

Adaptations: skeleton-only extraction, removal of attachment overlays, baking of
world transforms, uniform centering/scaling per bone, preservation of authored
leader geometry and relative positions, binary geometry packaging, Russian labels.
No landmarks have been projected to the project's reduced BodyParts3D geometry.

Latin labels: Z-Anatomy Resources/Translations0.txt, based on Terminologia Anatomica.
FIPAT. Terminologia Anatomica. 2nd ed., 2019, approved by IFAA 2020.
https://cdn.dal.ca/content/dam/dalhousie/pdf/library/FIPAT/TA2/FIPAT-TA2-Part-2.pdf
Individual anatomical terms are in the public domain. No FIPAT tables or definitions
are reproduced. Russian terminology is maintained in content/bone-landmarks-ru.tsv.

Coverage is PARTIAL: coverage.json records unannotated models. A source pointer
is an anatomical locator, not a segmented surface or a claim of expert validation.
Manual browser annotations are drafts tied to a specific bone and source hash.
