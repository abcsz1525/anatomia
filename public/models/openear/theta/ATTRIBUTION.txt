Changes: converted original PLY coordinates to a common normalized frame;
bone surface simplified by vertex clustering on a 0.4 mm grid; averaged normals;
internal segmented meshes converted without face simplification.
Russian labels, Latin labels, display colours and approximate canal-wall masks
were added locally. The masks select bone faces close to source segmentations;
they are not independently validated anatomical boundaries.
The specimen includes preparation cuts and adjacent fragments. It must not be
represented as a complete isolated temporal bone. Original source scan axes
are retained; they are not established anatomical display directions.
THETA is identified as left in the derivative supplier catalogue:
https://augment.surgery/products/theta-left-openear-3d-temporal-bones
This is not an independent anatomical sidedness verification.

Only two approximate wall regions have been transferred. The remaining
landmarks remain on the original complete-bone model.
Nerve, vessel and canal-volume meshes are not bone walls.
This adaptation is an educational prototype, not a validated clinical model.
